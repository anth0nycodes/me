## The trick

Every card appears a different height, so one fixed spacing does not work. Scale shrinks a card. Tilt shrinks it again, by the cosine of the angle. Perspective then makes the half leaning away look smaller than the half leaning toward you. I measure how tall each card actually appears and space them by that, so the visible gap stays at `12px` all the way out.

## What failed first

My first spacing only accounted for scale. Once I added the tilt, the cards looked shorter than the spacing assumed, and the gaps grew toward the ends.

## Details

- All the math is in card heights, which converts straight into a CSS `translate` percentage. The same code works at both card sizes, and only the `12px` gap is fixed.
- Position, scale and tilt take `1100ms`. The color change takes half that. It advances every `1200ms`, so each move settles just before the next one starts.
- The outermost card is faded out, which hides the jump when it wraps round to the other side.
- It pauses when it scrolls out of view, and it does not advance by itself under reduced motion.

## What I changed from the source

The original only scaled its cards. I added the tilt, for a 3D wheel effect, which is what made the spacing math necessary. I also used chevrons at the sides in place of the original's wing graphics.
